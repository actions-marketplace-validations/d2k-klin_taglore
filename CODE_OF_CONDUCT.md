# Code of conduct

Taglore follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep discussions about the work, and don't harass anyone. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that don't follow it, and may block people who repeatedly don't.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at hello@mr-d.dev. Reports are handled privately.
